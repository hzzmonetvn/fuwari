## What is KeyboxHub?

[KeyboxHub](https://keybox.hzzmonet.io.vn/) is a free website for people interested in Android device security and integrity research. It provides a place to learn about the topic and explore resources shared by the community.

## Use resources responsibly

Device credentials, signing keys, and certificates are sensitive security materials. Only use materials you own or are explicitly authorized to test. Do not use shared credentials to impersonate another device, bypass an app's security checks, or access services without permission. If you are developing an app, use official test tools and documented testing paths instead of credentials from unknown sources.

## Keep your own credentials safe

Never publish private keys, device-specific certificates, account credentials, or other secrets. If you believe a credential has been exposed, revoke or rotate it through the relevant official process. Verify the source and intended use of any resource before relying on it.

KeyboxHub is intended as a learning and research resource. Visitors are responsible for following applicable laws, platform policies, and the authorization boundaries of their testing.
